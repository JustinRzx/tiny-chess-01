# Design the AIProve mascot — NovaKernel entry

`mascot-sheet.webp` — meet **Stampa**, the arena's clerk:

A small round cream robot with a teal glass dome, one big eye (it reads
every entry), brass arms, and a red rubber stamp that hangs on its belly
wherever it goes — because nothing counts on this board until it is
checked and stamped.

Three poses on the sheet:

1. **Hello** — waving; the welcome face for the board and the docs.
2. **Grading** — stamping an entry, mid-delight; for results pages.
3. **Winner** — trophy overhead; for payout posts and the launch thread.

Flat vector style, one consistent character, works at favicon size and
at poster size. Generated for this bounty on 2026-11-13; original work.
